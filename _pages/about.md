I am a Senior Research Engineer at Google, where I work on agentic AI systems and trustworthy large language models. My research focuses on making LLM training data and model behavior measurable and accountable: data valuation, copyright-risk analysis, context steering, and synthetic data for safety evaluation.

My work has appeared at ICML, NAACL, and IEEE Transactions on Systems, Man, and Cybernetics: Systems. Before joining Google, I received an M.S. in Computer Science from Rice University and a B.E. in Computer Science from Wuhan University, with an exchange program at UC Berkeley. I am based in the San Francisco Bay Area.

I am always happy to connect about research, engineering, or collaboration. The best way to reach me is by [email](mailto:yanzhoupan@outlook.com), or find me on [GitHub](https://github.com/yanzhoupan), [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/yanzhou-pan), and [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=qG3yZeIAAAAJ).
